### 5. Master Visual Design Architecture (The Glass & Earth-Orange System)

The user interface represents the serene digital sanctuary of Master Wuji—translucent, contemplative, yet razor-sharp.

#### The Color & Translucency Blueprint
- **Glass Transparency Canvas:**
  - Background: Deep obsidian/cosmic slate (`#090D14` to `#0E131E`) layered with crystalline frosted glass surfaces (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`).
  - Blur Filter: `backdrop-blur-2xl` to `backdrop-blur-3xl` with specular perimeter highlights.
- **Vibrant Earthy Dark Orange Palette:**
  - Primary Earthy Orange: `#C2410C` (Terracotta / Earth Element Burnt Ochre)
  - Vibrant Earth Accent: `#EA580C` (Earthy Flame / Yang Spark Highlight)
  - Deep Amber Undertone: `#9A3412` (Smoky Burnt Ochre)
  - Subtle Ember Tint: `rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.08)` (Micro-glow & focus halos)
- **Crisp Lunar White:**
  - Pure White: `#FFFFFF` for razor-sharp typography, specular dots, and crystalline clarity.
  - Subdued White: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.40)` for secondary precision telemetry.

#### The "Small & Thin" Micro-Accent Directive
> **CRITICAL MANDATE:** Vibrant earthy dark orange and crisp white MUST ALWAYS remain **small, delicate, and thin**.
> - **NEVER** create bulky, thick orange buttons, heavy orange banners, or solid orange card fills.
> - **ALWAYS** apply vibrant earthy dark orange as razor-thin micro-accents:
>   - 1px hairline top highlights (`border-t border-orange-500/30`)
>   - 0.5px to 1px subtle card borders (`border border-white/10 hover:border-orange-500/30`)
>   - 1.5px SVG sparkline curves and meridian lines
>   - 2px status pulse pips and micro-pills
>   - 1.5px progress rings and thin percentage arcs
>   - Hairline cursor-aware glows (`radial-gradient` at `rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.04)`)
>   - Delicate icon outlines with ultra-fine stroke widths (1.5px)

#### The 3-Tier Scientific Method UX
1. **Tier 1: Observe (Wang Wen Wen Qie):**
   - Floating translucent diagnostic capsule (`rounded-[3rem]`, `backdrop-blur-3xl`, 1px razor-thin specular border).
   - Dual-input engine: High-resolution tongue / symptom photo analysis + natural language diagnostic inquiry.
   - Quick diagnostic channels: Tongue Analysis, 5 Elements, Horary Clock, Pulse Dynamics, Yao Shan.
2. **Tier 2: Understand (Bian Zheng & Meridian Matrix):**
   - Real-time diagnostic telemetry grid with 1.5px SVG sparklines, TCM syndrome differentiation, Yin-Yang ratio, and Five Element balance.
3. **Tier 3: Act (Yao Shan, Acupoints & Multimodal Synthesis):**
   - Curated intervention cards (Yao Shan dietary recipes, classical acupoints with exact anatomical locations, herbal tea formulas, Qi Gong breathwork).
   - Multimodal visual synthesis (AI botanical imagery and meditative movement videos).
   - Harmony momentum tracker with thin status pips.
